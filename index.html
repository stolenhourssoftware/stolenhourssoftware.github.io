The story

Stolen Hours Software is what happens in a Raleigh house after the kids are asleep: a FedEx dispatcher with a developer's past, building the software his family actually needs — in the margins of the day, one stolen hour at a time, with his AI partner Fred riding shotgun.

No investors, no roadmap decks, no growth hacks. Just useful things, made carefully, at night.
